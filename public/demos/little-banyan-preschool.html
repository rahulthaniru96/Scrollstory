Six little adventures, every day

Scroll through the day. Mornings are for focus, afternoons for being outdoors.

9:00

Art corner

Crayons, clay and big paper on the floor — nothing has to be perfect.

9:45

Music & rhymes

Telugu, Hindi and English rhymes with a real tabla and a xylophone.

10:30

Story time

One picture book, read slowly, then the children tell it back their way.

11:30

Garden

Each class has a small bed — they water it, weed it and eat what grows.

12:15

Blocks & numbers

Counting, sorting and building towers — maths before anyone calls it maths.

3:00

Splash & sand

Water play and the sand pit, with clothes changes packed by us.

Grows with your child

Small batches, the same class teacher all year, and a gentle settling-in week for every new child.

1½ – 2½ yrs

Playgroup

2½ – 3½ yrs

Nursery

Talking, listening, colours, shapes and first friendships.

3½ – 4½ yrs

LKG

Phonics, early numbers and lots of hands-on projects.

4½ – 5½ yrs

UKG

Reading, writing and getting ready for big school — calmly.
